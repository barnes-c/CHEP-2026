\section{Conclusion and Outlook}\label{conclusion}

This work presents an IaC framework that integrates Terraform/OpenTofu with CERN's existing provisioning and CM ecosystem. It introduces a declarative IaC integration layer that unifies infrastructure intent, provisioning, and service registration under a single version-controlled hood. Through custom providers for in-house services and CI/CD pipelines, the framework reduces boundary drift, improves traceability, and lowers reliance on undocumented operational knowledge. Especially in BC/DR and cross-environment replication scenarios.

Evaluation through real-world deployments and expert feedback confirmed that the framework improves operational predictability without disrupting established CM workflows. The separation of provisioning intent, execution, and runtime configuration ownership was shown to be a viable design for large, heterogeneous, and long-lived infrastructure environments such as CERN's.

Several directions for future work emerge naturally from this foundation. Tighter integration with GitOps-style reconciliation controllers would enable continuous drift correction for selected resources, extending the current operator-driven remediation model. Broader adoption is a gradual process, as teams familiar with existing workflows and without prior Terraform/OpenTofu experience will require time and support for an incremental migration path. Finally, incorporating Policy-as-Code enforcement as a mandatory design-time constraint would strengthen security and compliance guarantees across all managed resources.
