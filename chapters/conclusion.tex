\section{Conclusion and Outlook}\label{conclusion}

This work presented an IaC framework that integrates Terraform/OpenTofu with CERN's existing provisioning and CM ecosystem. Rather than replacing mature systems, the framework introduces a declarative IaC integration layer that unifies infrastructure intent, provisioning, and service registration under a single version-controlled hood. Through custom providers for CERN-specific services and standardised CI/CD pipelines, the framework reduces boundary drift, improves traceability, and lowers reliance on undocumented operational knowledge. Especially in BC/DR and cross-environment replication scenarios.

Evaluation through real-world deployments and expert feedback confirmed that the framework improves operational predictability and review confidence without disrupting established CM workflows. The separation of provisioning intent, execution, and runtime configuration ownership was shown to be a viable design for large, heterogeneous, and long-lived infrastructure environments such as CERN's.

Several directions for future work emerge naturally from this foundation. Tighter integration with GitOps-style reconciliation controllers would enable continuous drift correction for selected resource classes, extending the current operator-driven remediation model. Broader coverage of CERN's infrastructure platforms and legacy workflows, currently out of scope, would increase the practical reach of the framework. Finally, incorporating Policy-as-Code enforcement as a mandatory design-time constraint would strengthen security and compliance guarantees across all managed resources.
